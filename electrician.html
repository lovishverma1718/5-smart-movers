Infrastructure & Panel Upgrades

Modern luxury properties carry high load requirements from smart HVAC systems, heated pools, and high-speed EV chargers. We design and install whole-house panel upgrades, replacing outdated breakers with robust, code-compliant systems to secure your home's power grid.

  • Low-voltage track systems for art galleries
  • Smart wireless dimmer panels (Lutron, Caséta)
  • Low-voltage tree, pathway, and landscape spotlights

Our Methodology

System Progression

We operate under strict safety guidelines and structured steps to prevent panel faults and maintain full power integrity.

I
Step 01

Load Audit & Diagnostics

We analyze your home's total electrical footprint, calculating current usage, EV load tolerances, and diagnosing breaker box faults.

II
Step 02

System Layout Design

Our engineers draft panel schematics, calculate copper wiring gauges, coordinate utility shutdowns, and secure city permits.

III
Step 03

Installation & Safety Verify

Outdated boxes are removed, copper grids set, panel boxes grounded, surge protection locked, and final voltage testing checked for stability.
