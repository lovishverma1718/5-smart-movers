Custom Solutions

Routine Custodial Curation

Luxury estates demand ongoing maintenance to preserve expensive materials such as marble, granite, hardwoods, and architectural metals. Our highly trained, background-checked staff provides routine, detail-focused custodial services using non-abrasive premium formulations.

  • Specialized wood, metal, and stone surface polishing
  • Regular high-performance HEPA dust containment
  • High-level interior and exterior window clearing

Post-Renovation Detailing

Renovation and home building processes disperse heavy gypsum dust, paint splatters, and chemical residues across your spaces. Our deep construction detailing cleans baseboards, cleans moldings, details built-in cabinetry, and ensures a completely move-in ready finish.

  • Micro-dust removal from vents and ceilings
  • Safe paint, glue, and plaster residue extraction
  • Sanitization of fixtures, cabinetry, and kitchen spaces
Our Methodology

System Progression

We operate under careful guidelines to protect high-end materials and ensure client confidentiality.

I
Step 01

Spatial Audit & Plan

We inventory all delicate surfaces (Calacatta marble, mahogany, brass fixtures) and map custom organic, non-toxic agents to each material.

II
Step 02

HEPA Micro-Dusting

Using industrial HEPA filtration vacuums, our technicians trap microscopic airborne construction and environmental dust from ceilings, moldings, and trim.

III
Step 03

Polishing & Detail Hand-Wipe

Crews detail all metal fixtures, buff stone countertops, treat wood cabinetry with nourishing oils, and polish glass to absolute clarity.
