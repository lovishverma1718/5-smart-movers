Our Methodology

Relocation Timeline

From the initial consultation to the final box placement, we operate under a transparent, stress-free relocation framework.

I
Step 01

Estate Consultation

Our relocation managers visit your property to catalog items, evaluate packing requirements (e.g. fine art, glass surfaces), map access logistics, and draft a timeline plan.

II
Step 02

Custom Blueprint Planning

We develop custom blueprints mapping structural routes, packing logistics schedules, cataloging art/antique placements, and preparing transit coordinates.

III
Step 03

Custom Packing & Crating

Our crews construct customized wood crates on-site, apply anti-static wraps to tech equipment, and package all high-value antiques and fine art frame sets.

IV
Step 04

Climate-Controlled Transit

Relocation items are loaded systematically into our specialized climate-controlled fleet, secured with custom harnesses, and tracked live via satellite.

V
Step 05

Spatial Blueprint Setup

We unpack and place all spatial assets and custom furniture according to design blue-prints, carrying away all debris for immediate estate living.
