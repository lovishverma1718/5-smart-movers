Our Methodology

Renovation Process

A structured, transparent lifecycle from initial concept blueprint to final clean estate handover.

01

Discovery

Initial design consultation to outline layout opportunities, stylistic goals, and material preferences.

02

Design

Visualizing custom millwork, selecting stone slabs, and curating architectural lighting models.

03

Planning

Drafting complete costed structural blueprints, acquiring permits, and scheduling specialized crews.

04

Construction

Masking adjacent rooms, careful demolition, and precision build out by certified master tradesmen.

05

Final Walkthrough

Complete HEPA micro-dusting, final detailing inspection, and handover of your pristine wellness space.
